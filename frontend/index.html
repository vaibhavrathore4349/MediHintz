How to read your results

Our triaging system categorizes your symptoms into three simple levels.

✓ROUTINE CARE

Green

Your symptoms are mild and typical of common illnesses. Care is recommended, but not urgent.

  • Schedule at your convenience
  • Primary care physician
△PRIORITY CARE

Yellow

Your symptoms may be of concern and need attention within 24 hours.

  • Visit urgent care clinic
  • Schedule a telehealth visit
✦EMERGENCY

Red

Your symptoms require immediate life-threatening care. Seek immediate medical attention now.

  • Go to nearest ER
  • Call emergency services

When to seek emergency
help

Do not ignore these critical symptoms. Immediate medical attention is vital.

♡

Heart Attack

  • Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
  • Pain radiating to arm, back, or jaw
  • Shortness of breath with chest discomfort
✦

Stroke (FAST)

  • Face drooping on one side
  • Arm weakness or numbness
  • Speech difficulty or slurring
♧

Breathing Issues

  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Choking or blocked airway
  • Bluish color to lips or face
